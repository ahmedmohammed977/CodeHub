About CodeHub Academy

CodeHub – Build Your Future is a training academy made to help computer science students practice coding in a simple and clear way.

We focus on learning the basics first, like HTML5, CSS3, Python, and Linux, by building real projects in the lab.

In all our courses, we focus on three simple things:

Goal 1

1. Learn the Basics Well

We write clean and easy code so every student can understand and explain every line in the lab exam.

Goal 2

2. Practice in the Lab

Students work together in the computer lab to test their pages on desktop and mobile screens.

Goal 3

3. Simple & Responsive Design

We make sure all websites look organized and work well on phones, tablets, and computers.
