How to Ask for Help in Office Hours

If you have any problem in your code or project, do these steps:

  1. Check the instructor time on the Schedule page.
  2. Bring your laptop with your project folder open in VS Code.
  3. Come to Room B-204 10 minutes before the office hour starts.

What We Check in the Lab

In the lab discussion, instructors check:

  • Clean HTML tags and indentation.
  • How the page looks on mobile using F12 DevTools.
  • That you understand the CSS rules you wrote.
